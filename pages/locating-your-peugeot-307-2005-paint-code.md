## Unveiling Your Color Code's Location

**Where to Find Your Paint Code:** Your Peugeot 307 (2005) paint code is conveniently located on the driver's side front door jamb. Open the driver's door and look at the door pillar (the vertical part of the frame where the door latches).

An image here of an open driver's door of a Peugeot 307 (2005) with an arrow pointing to the general location of the paint code sticker.

![location](https://pinelaki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/WhatsApp-Image-2024-05-08-at-8.19.24-AM-1024x576.jpg)

## Decoding Your 307's Paint Code

**Identifying the Paint Code:** On the door jamb, you'll find a sticker with a bunch of information about your car. Look for a section labeled "Paint" or "Color" followed by a 3-letter code. In the case of our example car, the code is EZQC. This code is your key to matching your 307's original paint color perfectly.

![label](https://pinelaki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/WhatsApp-Image-2024-05-08-at-8.19.23-AM-1024x576.jpg)

**Understanding the Paint Code:** This simple code represents a specific formula for your Peugeot 307's paint color. Knowing this code is essential for buying the exact touch-up paint, ensuring your repairs blend seamlessly with the rest of the car.

## Caring for Your Car's Paint in Cyprus

Here are a few tips to keep your Peugeot 307 looking its best:

- **Regular washing:** Wash your car frequently with a good car shampoo to remove dust, dirt, and salt deposits.
- **Waxing:** Apply a good quality wax every few months to protect the paint and enhance shine.
- **Parking in the shade:** Whenever possible, park your car in the shade to minimize sun damage.
- **Car cover:** Use a car cover when parked outside for extended periods.
