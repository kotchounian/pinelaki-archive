# About Us

## Fast and reliable collision repair services

Pinelaki is your one-stop shop for fast and reliable collision repair services. We understand that being involved in an accident can be a stressful experience, so we strive to make the repair process as smooth and hassle-free as possible. Our team of experienced technicians is dedicated to providing high-quality repairs that meet or exceed industry standards. We use only the latest tools and equipment to ensure that your vehicle is restored to its pre-accident condition quickly and efficiently. Contact us today for a free estimate.

[Get a quote](https://pinelaki.com/contact-us/) ![collision-repair-paint-at-pinelaki](https://pinelaki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/collision-repair-paint-at-pinelaki-1.jpg)

Welcome to Pinelaki.com, where we've been at the forefront of the auto paint industry since 1991. For over three decades, we've dedicated ourselves to providing the highest quality touch-up paint solutions for a wide array of vehicles. Our journey began with a simple mission: to offer car enthusiasts and professionals alike the means to restore their vehicles to their original splendor with perfect color matches.

At Pinelaki, we understand that your vehicle is more than just a mode of transport; it's a reflection of your personal style and a source of pride. That's why we've spent years refining our formulae and processes to ensure that every product we offer meets the stringent requirements of our discerning clientele. From the everyday driver looking to touch up a few scratches to the classic car collector in search of rare color, our expansive inventory is designed to cater to every need.

Our founders, a team of passionate auto enthusiasts and paint technologists, launched Pinelaki to simplify the car paint selection process. They recognized early on the importance of matching the vehicle's original color as closely as possible, which led to the development of our innovative color-matching technology. This technology enables us to offer an unparalleled range of colors that precisely match the original manufacturers' specifications.

We've remained committed to innovation, quality, and customer satisfaction through the years. Our dedication to excellence is evident in every touch-up paint kit we produce and in the personalized service we offer to each of our customers. Whether you're a first-time visitor or a long-time patron, we're here to support you in making your vehicle look its best.

As a family-owned business, we cherish our relationships with our customers and the automotive community. We believe in going the extra mile to ensure your satisfaction, offering expert advice, and sharing our passion for cars with every interaction.

Thank you for choosing Pinelaki for your auto paint needs. We look forward to serving you and continuing to support car lovers everywhere with our quality products. Here's to many more years of bringing color and life back to vehicles around the world!

[Contact us](https://shop.pinelaki.com/contact-us/) to find the perfect match for your car today.
