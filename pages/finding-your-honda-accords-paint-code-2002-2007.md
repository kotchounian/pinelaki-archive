Today, we're diving deep into the world of Honda Accords, specifically the seventh generation models made between 2002 and 2007. If you've got one of these beauties and need to find your paint code, you might be in for a bit of a surprise. Unlike other Honda models that typically have the code on the driver's side door jamb, the seventh generation Accord likes to play hide-and-seek.

## Where to Look:

![Honda-Accord-paint-color-code-location](https://pinelaki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Honda-Accord-paint-color-code-location-1-1024x576.jpg)

Pop open the hood and head over to the left-hand side (driver's side). You'll find the color code sticker hiding near the suspension strut tower, usually on the strut itself. It's a small rectangular sticker, and the code will be clearly labeled.

## Example Code:

![Honda-Accord-paint-color-code-panel](https://pinelaki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Honda-Accord-paint-color-code-panel-1-1024x576.jpg)

In our case today, we're looking at a beautiful Accord with the code **NH623M**. This code tells us everything we need to know to perfectly match the paint.

## Why You Need Your Paint Code:

Knowing your paint code is crucial for a number of reasons:

- **Touch-ups:** Got a few pesky scratches or stone chips? No problem! With your paint code, you can easily order touch-up paint and keep your Accord looking pristine.
- **Body Repairs:** If you've had a fender bender or need any bodywork done, your paint code ensures a perfect match with the original factory finish.
- **Customizations:** Feeling adventurous? Maybe you want to add some racing stripes or a custom paint job. Your code is the starting point for any color matching.

## Protecting Your Paint in Cyprus:

Remember, the Cyprus sun can be harsh on your car's paint. UV rays, heat, and even dust can all take their toll. Here are a few tips to keep your Accord looking its best:

- **Regular Washing:** Wash your car regularly to remove dirt and grime that can damage the paint.
- **Waxing:** A good coat of wax provides a protective layer against the elements. [Book an appointment to wax your car](https://pinelaki.com/contact-us/)
- **Parking in the Shade:** Whenever possible, park in the shade to minimize sun exposure.
