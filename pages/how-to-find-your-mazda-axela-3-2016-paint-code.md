## Where to find your Mazda Axela paint code(pictures)

Unlike some other cars, finding the paint code on the **2016 Mazda Axela** is straightforward. You just need to open the **driver's side front door**. In Cyprus (and the UK, and other left-hand drive countries), this is the door on the right side of the car.

Once the door is open, look at the door jamb—the column where the door latches. You'll find a small sticker there with all the car's details. The paint code is usually listed as "COLOR" and is a two or three-digit alphanumeric code.

For example, a very common code for this model is **42A**, which is the code for the stunning Soul Red Metallic.

![Mazda-Axela-2016-panel-location](https://pinelaki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Mazda-Axela-2016-panel-location-1-1024x576.jpg)

## Why the correct code is a must-have

Using the correct paint code is the only way to get a perfect match for your car's original paint. Even if two cars look the same color, there can be subtle variations, and the sun can cause the color to fade over time. Using the exact code ensures your touch-up will be virtually invisible, keeping your car's finish looking flawless.

![Mazda-Axela-2016-color-panel-location](https://pinelaki.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Mazda-Axela-2016-color-panel-location-1-1024x576.jpg)

## Tips to keep your paint looking great in Cyprus

- **Wash regularly:** The salt and dust from our roads can damage your paint. A regular wash helps keep your car clean and protected.
- **Wax it up:** Applying a good car wax or sealant at least a couple of times a year adds a protective layer against UV rays and environmental damage.
- **Park smart:** Whenever possible, park in the shade to protect your paint from direct sun exposure.
