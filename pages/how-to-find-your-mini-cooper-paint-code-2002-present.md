Got a scratch or ding on your beloved Mini Cooper? Don't worry, it happens to the best of us! Whether you're a DIY enthusiast tackling a touch-up or planning a bigger repair, you'll need your Mini's paint code to ensure a perfect color match. This guide will show you exactly where to find it.

## Unveiling Your Colour Code's Secret Hiding Spot

![Mini-Cooper-2002-UP-color-code-location](https://pinelaki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Mini-Cooper-2002-UP-color-code-location-1-1024x576.jpg)

Unlike most cars that have the paint code on the door jamb, Mini likes to be a bit different. Here's where to find it:

1. **Open the Bonnet (Hood):** Pop open the bonnet and head over to the right-hand side (driver side Cyprus, UK, etc.).
2. **Peek Inside the Fender:** Look inside the fender, above the tyre (tire). You'll find a sticker on the inner fender wall.
3. **Locate the Code:** The sticker contains various information about your Mini, including the paint code. It might be labeled "Colour" or "Paint" and will typically be an alphanumeric code like "668/9".

## Decoding Your Mini's Paint Code

![Mini-Cooper-2002-UP-color-code-panel-location](https://pinelaki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Mini-Cooper-2002-UP-color-code-panel-location-1-1024x576.jpg)

This code is like your Mini's unique fingerprint for its paint colour. It tells you the exact shade and finish, ensuring you get a perfect match when ordering touch-up paint or having any bodywork done.
