## Description

## Features & Benefits

- **Glossy Finish:** Instantly enhances the appearance of touch-up repairs, restoring a showroom-quality shine.
- **Protective Layer:** Creates a durable barrier to protect your touch-up work from scratches, fading, and weathering.
- **Precision Application:** Brush applicator allows for precise targeting of small areas, ensuring flawless results.
- **Easy to Use:** Simple application process and fast drying time.
- **Extends Touch-Up Durability:** Maximizes the lifespan of your touch-up repairs.

## How to Use The Clear Coat Touch up Varnish

1. **Repair:** Clean and repair the damaged area using a matching touch-up paint.
2. **Wait:** Allow the touch-up paint to dry for approximately 10 minutes.
3. **Apply Clear Coat:** Use the brush applicator to carefully apply a thin layer of Touch-Up Clear Coat Varnish over the repaired area.
4. **Blend:** Gently blend the edges for a seamless and professional-looking finish.

## Important Note

- For best results, only apply the Touch-Up Clear Coat Varnish after the touch-up paint has dried for approximately 10 minutes.

## Compatibility

- Designed for use with most automotive touch-up paints.

## Additional Considerations

- **1K Formula:** While convenient and easy to use, 1K Clear Coat Varnish may be susceptible to damage from strong solvents like thinners or other industrial liquids. Avoid direct contact with these substances. To apply on larger areas consider our [1k Spray 500ml can](https://pinelaki.com/product/1k-clear-coat-varnish-spray-easy-protection-gloss-for-factory-code-paints/).
- **Compare to 2K:** For the ultimate in protection and durability, consider our [2K Clear Coat Varnish spray](https://pinelaki.com/product/2k-clear-coat-varnish-the-perfect-finish-to-apply-after-spray-paints/), designed for larger areas and maximum resistance.
