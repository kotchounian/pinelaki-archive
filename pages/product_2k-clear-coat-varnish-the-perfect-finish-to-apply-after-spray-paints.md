## Description

## Features & Benefits

- Enhances the brilliance and depth of Factory Code Spray Paints
- Provides a rock-solid protective layer
- Superior UV resistance to prevent fading and discoloration
- 2K formula with included hardener for exceptional durability
- Easy activation and application process
- Choose between high-gloss or matte finishes

## How to Use

1. **Prepare:** Ensure your Factory Code Spray Paint is completely dry (allow 15-20 minutes drying time at 20°C).
2. **Activate:** Break the internal seal to mix the clear coat with the hardener.
3. **Shake:** Shake the container vigorously for 2 minutes.
4. **Apply:** Apply the 2K Clear Coat Varnish in even coats, following recommended application guidelines.

## Important Notes

- For optimal results, apply the 2K Clear Coat only after the Factory Code Spray Paint has fully dried.
- Once activated, the 2K Clear Coat Varnish must be fully used within 10 hours. After this time, the mixture will harden and become unusable. (We have [1K clearcoat varnish](https://pinelaki.com/product/1k-clear-coat-varnish-spray-easy-protection-gloss-for-factory-code-paints/), which is usable for several months)
- Follow all safety precautions, including wearing a mask and working in a well-ventilated area.
