### FAQ

##### What is touch-up paint for?

Our custom-mixed touch-up paint is used for fixing minor, unsightly scratches and chips on your car's bodywork. It's a high-quality, DIY-friendly solution that restores your car's pristine appearance by perfectly matching its original factory color.

##### What car brands do you support?

We have universal compatibility and can mix paint for all major car and motorcycle brands. Our product page lists brands from Abarth to Zenvo, so whether you have a Ford, Toyota, BMW, or a Ferrari, we can match it.

##### How do I order the correct color?

When you place your order, you will need to select your car's make (brand) from the dropdown list and, most importantly, enter your exact "Paint Code" in the required field.

If you need assistence locating your car's color code then text us on Whatsapp or Viber at +357 99 228438

##### I don't know my car's paint code. How can I find it?

The paint code is essential for a perfect match. If you're not sure where to find it on your vehicle, you can check the guides on our blog or contact us directly for help, on Whatsapp or Viber at +357 99 228438.

##### What options can I choose from?

This specific product is a 35ml/50g touch-up bottle that comes with a convenient brush in the cap, making it incredibly simple to apply directly to scratches. For larger areas, we also offer a 400ml spray can, which is listed as a related product.

##### Do I need to buy anything else?

For the best results, we highly recommend applying a clear coat varnish over the touch-up paint. This adds gloss and provides a durable, protective layer to the repaired area. You can purchase this separately from our e-shop.

##### How do you deliver the paint?

We offer FREE delivery to any Akis Express location in Cyprus. We also provide affordable shipping to Greece and worldwide.
