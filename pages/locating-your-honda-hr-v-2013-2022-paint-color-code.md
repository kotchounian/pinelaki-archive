The secret to a seamless touch-up lies in finding your vehicle's original paint code. Let's dive in and discover where it's hiding!

## Unveiling Your Color Code's Location

![Color code location of the Honda on left side](https://shop.pinelaki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/IMG-20240416-WA0021-1-1024x576.jpg)

- **Where to Find Your Paint Code?** The key to your HR-V's paint code lies on the driver's side front door jamb. Simply open the driver's door and look closely at the pillar where the door latches when closed. \[Include a high-quality image of a Honda HR-V driver's side door open, with an arrow pointing to the general location of the panel\]

## Decoding Your Honda's Paint Code

![How the paint color panel looks like in Honda HR-V](https://shop.pinelaki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/IMG-20240416-WA0022-1-1024x576.jpg)

- **Identifying the Paint Code** You'll find a black panel adorned with the Honda logo and name – that's where your paint code resides. It will consist of 6 alphanumeric characters, for example, NH788P.

## Understanding the Paint Code

This unique code is the magic formula to obtaining the perfect touch-up paint for your Honda HR-V. Knowing this code unlocks the door to a flawless match, saving you from the headache of mismatched colors.
