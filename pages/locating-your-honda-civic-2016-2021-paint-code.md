Got a scratch or ding on your 10th generation Honda Civic (2016-2021)? Whether you're doing a DIY touch-up, tackling a bigger repair, or even ordering color-matched accessories, you'll need your car's paint code to ensure a perfect match. This guide makes finding it super easy!

## Unveiling Your Color Code's Location

**Here's where the paint code hides:**

**Where to Find Your Paint Code?** The paint code for your tenth-generation Honda Civic is conveniently located on the left side front door jamb. Simply open the left front door and look at the pillar where the door latches when closed.

![Locating-Your-Honda-Civic-(2016-2021)-Paint-Code (1)](https://pinelaki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Locating-Your-Honda-Civic-2016-2021-Paint-Code-1-1024x576.jpg)Image of a left door of a tenth-generation Honda Civic with an arrow pointing to the general location of the paint code sticker

1. **Open the left front side door.**
2. **Look at the door jamb** (where the door latches to the car frame).
3. **Find the sticker or metal plate.** It has a bunch of information about your Civic.
4. **Locate the code.** Look for a section labeled "COLOR" or "PAINT." The code might be all numbers, letters and numbers (like NH-737M), or even have two codes (one for the main body color and one for the trim). This is your Civic's unique paint identifier!

**Can't find it on the door jamb?** Check your owner's manual or under the hood.

## Decoding Your Civic's Paint Code

![Location-of-Honda-Civic-(2016-2021)-Paint-Code (1)](https://pinelaki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Location-of-Honda-Civic-2016-2021-Paint-Code-1-1024x576.jpg)

## Understanding the Paint Code

**Identifying the Paint Code** On the door jamb, you'll find a sticker or a metal plate containing various information about your car. Look for a section labeled "COLOR" or "PAINT," followed by an alphanumeric code (a combination of letters and numbers). For example: NH737M, This is your paint code!

This code is like a unique identifier for your Civic's exact paint color. Knowing this code is crucial for ordering the correct touch-up paint and ensuring a seamless blend with your car's original finish.
