# Pinelaki Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Welcome to Pinelaki. Your privacy and the security of your personal information are of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect from our users, how we use it, and the measures we take to protect it. By accessing or using our website and services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

#### Information Collection and Use

- **Personal Information**: When you place an order through our e-commerce shop, we collect personal information such as your name, address, and email. This information is used exclusively for the purpose of processing your order, shipping your purchased items, providing tracking information, and contacting you if needed for additional information regarding your order.
- **Payment Information**: All payment transactions are processed through third-party payment gateways, Stripe and PayPal. We do not collect or store any credit card or payment details on our website. Your payment information is directly provided to the respective payment processors, and their use of your personal information is governed by their privacy policies.
- **Analytics and Cookies**: We use third-party analytics tools, including Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Pinterest Tag, Microsoft Clarity, and others, to collect information about website traffic and user interactions. These tools help us understand how visitors use our site, allowing us to improve our services and user experience. The data collected through these analytics tools may include your IP address, browser type, operating system, referral source, and page views.

#### Data Protection

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

#### Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of the personal information that we hold. If you wish to inquire about or exercise these rights, please contact us at info@pinelaki.com using the email address associated with your account.

#### Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites that are not operated by us. We strongly advise you to review the privacy policy of every site you visit. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

#### Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. We advise you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

#### Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please [contact us](https://pinelaki.com/contact-us/).
