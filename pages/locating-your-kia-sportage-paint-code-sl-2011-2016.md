A scratch or chip on your beloved Kia Sportage SL (2011-2016) doesn't have to be a permanent blemish. With the right touch-up paint, you can restore your SUV's finish to its original glory. The key to this transformation lies in your vehicle's specific paint code. Let's uncover where it's hidden and how to decipher it.

## Unveiling Your Color Code's Location

![Showing the color code location of Kia Sportage](https://pinelaki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/IMG-20240425-WA0014-1-1024x576.jpg)

- **Where to Find Your Paint Code?** The paint code for your Kia Sportage SL (2011-2016) is conveniently located on the Left side front door jamb (passenger side in Cyprus). Open your door and look at the pillar where it would latch when closed.

## Decoding Your Kia's Paint Code

- **Identifying the Paint Code** You'll find a black panel with the Kia name and logo. This panel houses your paint code, which will consist of 2-3 characters. It could be a combination of letters and numbers (e.g., D5 or GYB).

![Showing the color code panel of Kia Sportage](https://pinelaki.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/IMG-20240425-WA0013-1-1024x576.jpg)

## Understanding the Paint Code

This seemingly simple code holds the secret to your Kia Sportage SL's unique paint color. Knowing this code ensures you get the perfect match for your touch-up paint, allowing for a seamless repair.
